## What this edition is

The current English version of the Evidence-Based Management Guide, released on 7 May 2024.
Scrum.org states that the previous version remains available in other languages until each is
replaced by the 2024 one, so the language a reader picks determines which version they get.

Evidence-Based Management is an extension rather than an implementation of Scrum. It does not
describe how to do Scrum and makes no claim to be Scrum; it concerns the value an organisation
realises from its products, and how to measure it in order to decide what to improve. That
concern sits outside what the Scrum Guide addresses.
