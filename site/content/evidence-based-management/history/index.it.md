Scrum.org states that the Evidence-Based Management Guide was released in 2015 and updated in
2018, 2020 and 2024, which makes four versions in all. Only the 2024 release carries a date on
the publishers' pages — 7 May 2024 — so it is the only edition presented here. The 2015, 2018
and 2020 versions are recorded as existing, but no publication month for them is stated at
source, and this site does not date an edition it cannot verify.
