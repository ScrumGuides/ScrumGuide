guide_whatis: |
  **If you read one thing, read this.** Start here, then read The Scrum Guide.

  An implementation guide, and the one to begin with. Scrum Guide 2026 states what each
  element of Scrum must achieve and deliberately says nothing about how. This guide is the
  how: the timeboxes, the team size, the agenda of each gathering, the techniques a team
  actually runs. Two teams can both be doing Scrum correctly and differ on every page of
  this guide. Neither can differ on The Scrum Guide.

  Its text is the November 2020 Scrum Guide by Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, reproduced
  under CC BY-SA 4.0. That edition is the most widely taught description of Scrum in
  practice, so it is the best starting point a team can have. This site is not published by
  them, and the canonical source is [scrumguides.org](https://scrumguides.org/).
