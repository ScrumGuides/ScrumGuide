guide_whatis: |
  **Draft.** This guide has not been reviewed or approved. Read it as a proposal.

  An implementation guide, for a team of three to five people building a product in which
  most production work is carried out by AI tooling under the direction of the people on the
  team. Scrum Guide 2026 states what each element of Scrum must achieve and deliberately
  says nothing about how. This guide is one how: the team size, the Cycle length, the
  cadence and timebox of each loop, and what closes it.

  It differs from [Getting Started for Scrum Teams](/getting-started-for-scrum-teams/) in the
  ratio it is built for. The mechanics most teams run were settled when producing work was
  expensive and inspecting it was cheap. For this team the ratio is reversed, and the
  mechanics move with it.

  Implementation guides add guidance for a particular context. They may add to the parent
  guide's conditions and may constrain them further. They may not override or weaken them.
