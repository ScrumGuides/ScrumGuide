## What this edition is

The substantive revision of the early period, and the edition that gives the guide the title and subtitle it still carries. The publishers' own revision history calls it "a significant update with many changes" and sorts them into readability and substance.

On their account the substantive changes removed Release Planning, Release Burndown, the Product and Sprint Backlog Burndowns and the pigs-and-chickens metaphor; replaced committing to scope with a forecast; renamed the Team the Development Team; made the Product Backlog ordered rather than prioritised; defined the Sprint Backlog; and refined team size.

The site's specification originally listed the edition set as 2010.2 → 2011.10, omitting this one. It is here because the publishers' own revision history names it as a release in its own right.
