Translations are the work of the translators credited against each language by the
publishers. They are not reproduced here: every link below goes to the publishers' own
file, which is the version the translators and the publishers maintain.

If you find an error in a translation, raise it with that translator or with the
publisher, not here.

The official directory, with the translator credited for each language, is at
[scrumguides.org](https://scrumguides.org/download.html).
