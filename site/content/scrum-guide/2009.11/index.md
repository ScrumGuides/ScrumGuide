## What this edition is

The earliest document Scrum.org published under this name, and the start of the lineage this site presents. Its cover reads *Scrum Guide*; the February 2010 edition that followed it is titled simply *Scrum*.

The authors' own later guides date the first Scrum Guide to 2010 — the November 2020 edition opens "We wrote the first version of the Scrum Guide in 2010" — and the publishers' public revision history begins with the changes between 2010 and 2011. This edition is presented here because the document exists and carries the name, not because the authors count it as the first.
